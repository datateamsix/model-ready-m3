# Logo Production Specification

## 1. Master mark intent

The PreM3 mark represents fragmented input resolving into a structured, model-ready system. It must retain the asymmetry and progression of the approved artwork rather than becoming a generic isometric cube.

### Composition rules

- Trailing fragments stay on the left side of the mark.
- Trailing fragments use light/cool gray only.
- The structural body is predominantly Deep Navy.
- Indigo is a controlled transition accent, primarily on the top/transition plane.
- The right/resolved face is Cyan.
- Maintain open white channels between the structural tiles.
- Preserve the approved perspective and apparent depth.
- Do not add gradients, strokes, drop shadows, bevels, or 3D rendering to the master mark.

**Positioning must be matched to the approved reference image, not reconstructed from these verbal rules alone.**

## 2. Wordmark

- Text: `PreM3`
- `PreM`: Deep Navy `#1A1F4B`
- `3`: Cyan `#00C2F5`
- Weight: bold/heavy, matching the approved board.
- Export the approved wordmark as vector outlines/paths.
- Do not render the official wordmark using live browser text.

## 3. Divider

The primary horizontal lockup includes the thin vertical divider shown in the approved reference. Preserve its visual weight and spacing. Use a light cool neutral derived from the brand grays.

## 4. Tagline

Official lockup tagline: `MAP. MEND. MODEL.`

- uppercase
- generous tracking
- MAP / MEND in navy or restrained neutral
- MODEL in Cyan
- tagline is optional below the minimum deployment size defined in the small-size spec

## 5. Clear space

Until the final vector master is measured, use a conservative clear-space rule of **1× the cap-height of the `M` in `PreM3`** around the full lockup. Once the vector is finalized, encode the measured rule in Figma and update this file if necessary.

## 6. Backgrounds

- Preferred: full-color logo on white / very light neutral.
- Dark surface: approved inverse lockup only.
- Do not place the full-color mark on saturated photography or low-contrast backgrounds without a controlled container.

## 7. Forbidden modifications

Do not:
- redraw with CSS grid/flex tiles;
- use a generic cube icon;
- auto-trace and ship without manual vector cleanup;
- randomize tile colors;
- change the number or location of trailing fragments casually;
- stretch, skew, rotate, or compress;
- add outlines, glows, shadows, or gradients to the master artwork;
- recolor the numeral `3` away from the approved Cyan in the primary identity.
